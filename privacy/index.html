Privacy Policy

Saturn Games & Apps. Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play mobile games published by Saturn Games & Apps (the "Games"), including Perfect Hotel Sim, Cannons And Pirates, Kick Up!, Slime Dash and TowerMaker, and how it is used.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or any account. Game progress (levels, money, unlocked items and settings) is stored only on your device.

Advertising

The Games are free and show ads. Ads are delivered by Google AdMob and, in some Games, through the CAS.AI mediation platform (Clever Ads Solutions) and the ad networks it works with, such as AppLovin, Unity Ads, Mintegral, Pangle, IronSource and Meta Audience Network. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, these partners may collect:

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, language, screen size
  • The advertising ID of your device (Google Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Information about ad interactions: ads shown, clicks, video completions
  • Diagnostic data such as crash and performance information of the ad SDKs

Each partner processes this data under its own privacy policy. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services, Google's advertising policies and, for CAS.AI and its partners, cas.ai/privacy-policy.

Game analytics

Some Games, including Perfect Hotel Sim, use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the Game is played so we can improve it: for example which levels players reach, which rewards they collect and which optional ad offers they accept. Firebase collects app usage events, an app instance identifier, device information (model, operating system, language) and approximate location derived from the IP address. It does not receive your name or contact details. We have turned off sharing this data with Google for its own products, benchmarking, technical support and account specialists. Data is processed under Firebase's privacy terms.

In-app purchases

Some Games may offer optional in-app purchases. Payments are handled by Google Play; we never receive your card or payment details. We only learn which item was bought so the Game can deliver it.

Your choices and consent

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the Games ask for your consent before personalised ads are shown. Where a Game has a privacy button in its settings, you can change your choice there at any time.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Residents of California and other US states can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for advertising using the same device settings.

Children

The Games are not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a Game, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and deletion

We do not keep personal data on our own servers. Uninstalling a Game removes all game data from your device. To request deletion of data held by an advertising partner, use the partner's own privacy controls or contact us and we will forward your request.

Security

Data sent by the ad SDKs is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
